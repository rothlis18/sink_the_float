\[ R = \frac{1}{N_s + B_c} \]

**Mechanism Overview:**
Tokenized transaction barriers require users to pay a fee to query data, effectively creating a pay-to-query model. This fee is denoted as \( F_q \).

**Mathematical Model:**
The cost of querying data \( C_q \) is:
\[ C_q = F_q \cdot Q \]
where \( Q \) is the amount of data queried.

**Barrier Effect:**
The barrier effect \( E_b \) is defined as:
\[ E_b = \frac{C_q}{Q} = F_q \]
A higher \( F_q \) increases the barrier effect, making data access more restrictive.
