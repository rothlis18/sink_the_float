**Mathematical Constraint Loop:**

1. **Actuarial Discount Rate and Risk-Asset Allocation:**

   - Let \( V \) be the value of the pension fund's liabilities.
   - Let \( A \) be the value of the pension fund's assets.
   - The fund's financial health is determined by the equation:
     \[
     V = A \times \frac{1 - (1 + D)^{-n}}{D}
     \]
     where \( n \) is the number of years until the liabilities are fully funded.

2. **Risk-Asset Allocation:**
   - Let \( R \) be the return on risk assets (e.g., public equity).
   - Let \( F \) be the float (cash) available for investment.
   - The fund must allocate \( F \) to risk assets to meet the required return on liabilities:
     \[
     F \times R = V \times D
     \]
   - Solving for \( R \):
     \[
     R = \frac{V \times D}{F}
     \]

   - If \( I \) is high, the value of public equity decreases, leading to:
     \[
     R = \frac{V \times D}{F} \times \frac{1}{1 + I}

     \[
     \frac{V \times D}{F} \times \frac{1}{1 + I} > \frac{V \times D}{F}
     \]
   - This inequality is always true, but it highlights the structural pressure to allocate more float to risk assets to compensate for inflation.
